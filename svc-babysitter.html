Frequently Asked Questions
Are babysitters background verified?›
Yes — all babysitters and nannies are police-verified and background-checked before onboarding. Verification documents can be requested at booking.
Can I get the same babysitter every time?›
Yes — for regular bookings we assign a consistent caregiver so your child builds comfort and trust with one person.
What if my child has allergies or medical conditions?›
Mention all allergies, conditions, and special instructions when booking. A caregiver with appropriate experience will be assigned. Medical care or giving medicines is not included.
Can the babysitter take my child to the park or outside?›
Yes — but only with your prior written or verbal approval. Mention this in your booking notes and the caregiver will confirm before taking the child outside.
I have two children — is that okay?›
Yes — set the number of children when booking. A surcharge of ₹300/day applies for 2+ children. For 3+ children, contact us for a custom quote.
What is the difference between a babysitter and a nanny?›
A babysitter provides general childcare and supervision. An experienced nanny provides structured activities, developmental focus, potty training support, homework help, and follows a more planned routine.
Is overnight babysitting available?›
Yes — select "Overnight Babysitter" for night coverage from 10 PM to 8 AM. The caregiver handles night feeds, settling, and the morning routine.
What if the babysitter is not a good fit for my child?›
Your child's comfort is our priority. If the caregiver is not a good fit, we replace them within 24 hours at no extra charge — no questions asked.
