Frequently Asked Questions
Is pest control safe for children and pets?›
Yes — all chemicals used are WHO-certified and government-approved. We use odourless gel baits in kitchen and food areas. Re-enter after 1–2 hours once the chemical has dried.
Do I need to vacate my home during treatment?›
Yes — for spray treatments, all family members and pets should vacate for 1–2 hours. Gel bait treatment requires no vacating. The technician will advise before starting.
How long does the treatment last?›
Cockroach and ant gel: 1–3 months. Mosquito spray: 1 month. Termite treatment: 3–5 years. Results depend on cleanliness, entry point control, and seasonal activity.
What if pests return after treatment?›
All treatments come with a 30-day warranty. If pests return within 30 days, the technician revisits and re-treats at no extra charge.
Do I need to prepare anything before the technician arrives?›
Clear kitchen counters and store food in closed containers. Move furniture slightly away from walls if possible. Cover aquariums and bird cages. The technician will send specific instructions before the visit.
How many visits are included?›
Cockroach, bed bug, and rodent treatments include a follow-up visit. Other treatments are single-visit. The 30-day warranty covers re-treatment if pests return.
Can you do pest control for my office or shop?›
Yes — select "Office / Shop" as the property size. For large commercial properties (factories, warehouses, restaurants), contact us directly for a custom quote.
How do I prevent pests from coming back?›
The technician will share specific prevention tips after treatment — sealing entry points, fixing leaky pipes, proper food storage, and regular cleaning are the key preventive steps.
