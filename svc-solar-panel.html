Frequently Asked Questions
How often should solar panels be cleaned?›
Every 3–6 months in most areas. In dusty, coastal, or high-pollution areas, every 2–3 months is recommended to maintain peak output.
Will cleaning damage the solar panels?›
No — our technicians use soft water wash and non-abrasive cleaning tools specifically designed for solar panels. This will not scratch the glass or void your panel warranty.
My solar output has reduced — what should I book?›
Book "System Inspection & Health Check" first. The technician will identify whether it's a dirty panel issue, inverter fault, hot spot, or wiring problem and advise accordingly.
Are solar panels and inverters included in the service price?›
No — the prices shown are labour/service charges only. Solar panels, inverters, batteries, cables, and mounting structures are priced separately based on brand and capacity.
Can you help with government subsidy (PM Surya Ghar)?›
Our technicians can guide you on subsidy eligibility and the process. However, actual subsidy application and DISCOM paperwork is not included in the standard service charges.
Which inverter and panel brands do you service?›
All major brands — Luminous, Adani, Waaree, Tata Power Solar, Havells, Su-Kam, Delta, Fronius, SMA, and all others commonly used in India.
Is there a warranty on the service work?›
Yes — 30-day service warranty on all repair and installation work. If the same fault reoccurs within 30 days due to workmanship, a technician revisits at no extra charge.
How long does solar panel cleaning take?›
Up to 4 panels: 45–60 minutes. Up to 8 panels: 1.5 hours. Larger systems (10+ panels): 2–3 hours. Full installation: 4–6 hours depending on system size.
