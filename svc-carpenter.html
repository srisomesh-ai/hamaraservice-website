Frequently Asked Questions
Does the carpenter bring wood and materials?›
Carpenters bring all tools and basic hardware like screws and hinges. For wood, plywood, or custom materials, cost is confirmed before purchasing — you can also provide your own materials.
Can the carpenter assemble IKEA or flat-pack furniture?›
Yes — select "Furniture Assembly". The carpenter will assemble any flat-pack furniture from IKEA, Pepperfry, Urban Ladder, Amazon, or any other brand.
My door is dragging on the floor — can that be fixed?›
Yes — select "Door Repair / Adjustment". The carpenter will plane the bottom of the door or adjust the hinges to fix the dragging.
Can the carpenter do custom woodwork?›
Yes — select "Custom Woodwork / Other" and describe your requirement in the booking notes. The carpenter will assess and give a quote before starting.
Is painting or polishing included?›
No — painting, wood staining, and polishing are separate services. Book a painter for those requirements.
How long does carpentry work take?›
Simple repairs (door, hinge, lock): 30–60 minutes. Furniture assembly or shelf fitting: 1–2 hours. Complex or multiple jobs: 2–4 hours.
Is there a guarantee on the work?›
Yes — 7-day workmanship guarantee. If the same fault reoccurs within 7 days due to the carpenter's work, the revisit is free.
Can I book for multiple carpentry tasks in one visit?›
Yes — select all the work you need when booking. The carpenter completes all tasks in a single visit, making it more cost-effective.
