Frequently Asked Questions
Does the professional bring cleaning supplies?›
With Materials: Yes — all descaling agents, scrubbing pads, acid cleaner, and disinfectants are brought. Without Materials: You provide everything. Choose your service type above.
How long does it take per bathroom?›
45 minutes to 1.5 hours per bathroom depending on size and condition. Very dirty or mold-affected bathrooms may take longer.
Can heavy hard water stains be removed?›
Yes. Professional-grade descaling acid is used for stubborn mineral deposits on tiles, taps, and shower heads.
How often should bathroom deep cleaning be done?›
Every 1–2 months for regular maintenance. If mold or heavy staining is present, clean immediately and then maintain monthly.
Can mold and fungus be completely removed?›
Surface mold and fungus can be cleaned and treated. Deeply embedded mold in grout may require multiple sessions. Select "Mold / fungus present" above for appropriate treatment.
