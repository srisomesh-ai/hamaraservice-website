Frequently Asked Questions
Does the technician bring tools?›
Yes. All cleaning tools, coil cleaner, and drain flush solution are brought.
Will the AC cool better after cleaning?›
Yes. Most customers notice improved cooling within hours — typically 1–3°C improvement.
How often should AC be cleaned?›
At least once before summer (March–April) and once mid-season.
Is outdoor unit cleaning included?›
Yes. Condenser coil cleaning and exterior wash of the outdoor unit is included.
Do I need to switch off the power?›
Please ensure the AC main switch is accessible. The technician handles the rest.
