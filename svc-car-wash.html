Frequently Asked Questions
How long does a car wash take?›
Basic exterior wash: 30–45 minutes. Interior + exterior: 1–1.5 hours.
Is waterless car wash available?›
Some providers offer waterless or low-water eco-friendly washing — suitable for apartment parking.
Is doorstep wash available in apartments?›
Yes — washer comes to your parking spot, whether basement or open.
Why does car type affect the price?›
Larger vehicles like SUVs and luxury cars require more time, water, and materials — a small surcharge applies.
How often should I wash my car?›
Every 1–2 weeks depending on usage and weather. Dusty areas may need more frequent washing.
