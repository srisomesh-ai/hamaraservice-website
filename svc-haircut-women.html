Frequently Asked Questions
Is the beautician female?›
Yes — all beauty experts for women's services are verified, trained female professionals.
Does the beautician bring all products?›
Yes — all tools, wax, facial kits, nail care products, and makeup are included. Hair colour is the only exception — arrange in advance or confirm with the beautician.
Can I book multiple services in one visit?›
Yes — select as many services as you need. The beautician will complete them all in a single visit.
Is waxing done hygienically at home?›
Yes — fresh wax and new applicators are used for every client. Rica wax option is available for sensitive skin.
Can I book for a wedding or function?›
Yes — select "Bridal / Party Makeup" and choose the makeup type. Add saree draping, bridal hair styling, or mehendi as add-ons for a complete bridal package.
Do you offer keratin or hair smoothening?›
Keratin, rebonding, and smoothening are specialised treatments not included in this service. Contact us directly for availability and pricing.
What if I'm not happy with the service?›
Contact us within 24 hours and we will arrange a free correction visit or a full refund as appropriate.
How far in advance should I book for a bridal event?›
For bridal bookings, we recommend booking at least 3–5 days in advance to ensure the best beautician match for your requirements.
