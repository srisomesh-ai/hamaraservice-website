Frequently Asked Questions
How long does furniture or carpet cleaning take?›
Usually: Sofa cleaning 1–3 hours · Mattress cleaning 30–60 minutes · Carpet cleaning 1–4 hours depending on size.
How long does it take to dry?›
Typically 2–6 hours depending on fabric type and weather conditions. Do not sit or use until fully dry.
Can all stains be removed?›
Most stains can be treated successfully, but old or permanent stains may remain partially visible.
Is steam cleaning safe for fabrics?›
Yes, professionals use suitable cleaning methods based on fabric type — no damage to delicate materials.
Do cleaning professionals bring equipment?›
Yes, all required machines, shampoos, and tools are usually provided by the professional.
How often should carpets and sofas be deep cleaned?›
Recommended every 6–12 months depending on usage and pets.
Will cleaning remove bad odors?›
Yes, deodorizing treatment helps remove most common odors effectively.
Is the service safe for children and pets?›
Yes, most professional services use safe and approved cleaning products.
