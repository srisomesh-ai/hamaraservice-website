Frequently Asked Questions
How often should a water purifier be serviced?›
Ideally every 6–12 months. If you notice slow flow, bad taste, or reduced output, service immediately regardless of schedule.
How often should filters be changed?›
Pre-filters and sediment filters: every 3–6 months. Carbon filters: every 6–12 months. RO membrane: every 2–3 years. The technician will advise based on your water quality.
Is the cost of filters included?›
No — filters, membrane, and UV lamp are priced separately based on brand and model. The technician confirms the exact cost before replacing anything.
What does TDS mean and why does it matter?›
TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) measures dissolved minerals and impurities in water. The ideal TDS for drinking water is 50–150 ppm. Our technician checks this and tells you where your water stands.
Which brands do you service?›
All major brands — Kent, Aquaguard, Pureit (HUL), Livpure, Blue Star, Havells, Eureka Forbes, AO Smith, Whirlpool, and more.
My water has a strange taste — what service should I book?›
Book "Bad Taste / Smell in Water". Usually caused by an expired carbon filter — the technician will diagnose and replace if needed.
Is there a warranty on service work?›
Yes — 30-day service warranty on all labour work. If the same issue reoccurs within 30 days, the technician revisits at no extra charge.
How long does the service take?›
Routine service: 45–60 minutes. Filter replacement: 30–45 minutes. Repair: 1–2 hours depending on the fault.
