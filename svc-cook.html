Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need to provide groceries?›
Yes — the cook brings only cooking skill and experience. All ingredients — vegetables, dal, rice, spices, oil — should be available in your kitchen before the cook arrives.
Can I get the same cook every day?›
Yes — for regular daily bookings we assign the same cook so they learn your taste and preferences. Consistency is especially maintained for monthly arrangements.
Can the cook follow my specific diet or recipe?›
Yes — mention dietary requirements at booking. Less oil, no garlic-onion, diabetic-friendly, high-protein, specific recipes — the cook will follow your instructions.
Does the cook clean the kitchen after cooking?›
Yes — the cook washes the utensils used, wipes the kitchen platform, and turns off the gas before leaving. Full kitchen cleaning is not included.
Can I book a cook for a party of 20 people?›
The party cooking package covers up to 20 guests. For larger gatherings, contact us for a custom party catering quote with multiple cooks.
What cuisines can the cook prepare?›
North Indian, South Indian, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Punjabi, and general Indian home cooking. Mention your cuisine preference when booking so the right cook is assigned.
What if I'm not happy with the cook's cooking?›
Your satisfaction is our priority. If you're not happy with the cook, we will replace them within 24 hours — no questions asked.
Is the cook verified and trustworthy?›
Yes — all cooks are background-checked and verified before joining. For added safety, you can request the cook's ID documents at booking.
