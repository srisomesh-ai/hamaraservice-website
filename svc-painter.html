Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need to buy the paint?›
Yes. You purchase the paint of your chosen brand and colour. The painter brings all tools.
How much paint do I need?›
Roughly 1 litre of emulsion covers 100 sq ft per coat. The painter can advise after measuring.
Is wall putty included?›
Basic putty touch-up may be included. Full putty work is generally charged separately.
Do painters clean the area after work?›
Yes. Basic cleanup is included after painting is complete.
Is exterior painting available?›
Yes. Both interior and exterior painting services are available.
