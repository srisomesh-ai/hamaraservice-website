Frequently Asked Questions
Which CCTV cameras are commonly installed?›
Dome cameras, Bullet cameras, Wi-Fi cameras, PTZ cameras, and Night vision cameras are all commonly supported.
Can CCTV cameras be viewed on mobile phones?›
Yes, most modern CCTV systems support remote mobile viewing. The technician sets up the app and remote access on your phone.
Is internet required for CCTV cameras?›
Internet is needed for remote viewing. Local recording to DVR/NVR works without internet — you can view footage on-site anytime.
How long is CCTV footage stored?›
Storage duration depends on hard disk size and recording settings — usually 7–30 days. Ask the technician for advice on storage capacity.
Are wiring and installation materials included?›
Basic installation materials may be included. Additional wiring, long cable runs, or special accessories may cost extra — confirmed before work starts.
Can old CCTV systems be repaired or upgraded?›
Yes, technicians can often repair existing cameras, upgrade DVR/NVR, or add cameras to an existing system.
Is night vision available in CCTV cameras?›
Yes, most CCTV cameras include infrared night vision support — confirm with the brand before purchasing.
Is there warranty on installation services?›
Many providers offer limited warranty on installation work and supported devices. Ask your technician for specific terms.
