Frequently Asked Questions
Does the therapist bring oil and towels?›
Yes — massage oil, clean towels, and a portable mat are all brought by the therapist. You just need a clean, private room.
Can I request a female therapist?›
Yes — female therapists are available for female customers. Select "Female therapist" preference when booking. Availability may vary by location.
Is massage safe for seniors (60+)?›
Yes — our therapists are trained for senior-specific techniques. Gentle pressure and specific care is taken for the 60+ age group. Inform the therapist of any medical conditions before the session.
What is the difference between Swedish and Deep Tissue?›
Swedish massage uses long gentle strokes for relaxation. Deep tissue targets deeper muscle layers and knots — better for chronic pain or tension. If unsure, Swedish is the safest starting choice.
Can I book for 2 people simultaneously?›
Yes — set the number of people to 2 or more. A separate therapist comes for each person. Additional persons are charged at the per-person rate.
What should I do before and after the session?›
Avoid heavy meals 1 hour before. Wear comfortable, loose clothing. Drink water after the session. Rest for at least 30 minutes after a deep tissue massage.
Is Ayurvedic massage different from Swedish?›
Yes — Ayurvedic (Abhyanga) uses warm herbal oil and follows traditional marma point techniques for detox, circulation, and energy balancing. It is more therapeutic than relaxation-focused Swedish.
