Frequently Asked Questions
How long does kitchen cleaning take?›
Usually 2–5 hours depending on kitchen size and grease buildup.
Are cleaning chemicals food-safe?›
Yes, professional services generally use safe and approved cleaning products suitable for kitchen environments.
Will oil and grease stains be removed completely?›
Most grease and stains can be removed significantly, but old permanent stains may not disappear fully.
Do I need to empty kitchen cabinets?›
For deep interior cleaning, it is recommended to empty cabinets beforehand for best results.
Is chimney cleaning included?›
Only external chimney cleaning is usually included unless full chimney servicing is booked separately.
How often should kitchen deep cleaning be done?›
Recommended every 2–3 months to maintain hygiene and prevent heavy grease buildup.
Do professionals bring cleaning equipment?›
Yes, most services bring their own tools, chemicals, and machines. For without-material bookings, you provide the supplies.
