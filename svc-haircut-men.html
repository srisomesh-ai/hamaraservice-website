Frequently Asked Questions
Does the barber bring all tools?›
Yes — scissors, clippers, trimmer, fresh blades, shaving foam, aftershave, neck strips, and a hair cape are all included. You only need to provide a chair.
Can I get a fade or designer haircut at home?›
Yes — select "Fade / Taper" or "Layer / Designer" from the cut style options after selecting Haircut. Our barbers are experienced with modern styles.
Can I book for multiple people in one visit?›
Yes — set the number of people in the sub-panel after selecting Haircut or Combo. The barber will attend to everyone one by one in a single visit.
Is it safe and hygienic?›
Yes — barbers use a fresh blade for every customer and sanitize all tools before each visit. This is actually more hygienic than shared salon equipment.
Can I book a barber for my child?›
Yes — select "Kids Cut" from the cut style options. Our barbers are patient and experienced with young children.
Do I need to provide anything?›
Just a chair and good lighting. The barber brings everything else. If you want hair colour applied, arrange the colour product in advance or confirm with the barber.
What if I'm not happy with the haircut?›
The barber will adjust or correct the style before leaving. If you are still not satisfied, contact us within 24 hours and we will arrange a free correction visit.
How long does a home haircut visit take?›
Haircut only: 20–30 min. Haircut + beard: 40–50 min. Full combo: 60–90 min. Multiple people: add 20–30 min per additional person.
