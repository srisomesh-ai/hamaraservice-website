💳 Refund Policy

Our commitment to fair and transparent refunds

Effective: June 2025

Overview

At HamaraService, we are committed to ensuring complete customer satisfaction. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds are provided for services booked through our platform.

📞 For urgent refund queries, contact us at support@hamaraservice.com or call +91 98765 43210 — we respond within 4 business hours.

Eligible Refund Scenarios

✅ Full Refund (100%)

  • Service provider did not show up within 60 minutes of the scheduled time
  • Booking cancelled at least 24 hours before the scheduled service time
  • Service could not be completed due to a fault on the provider's side
  • Duplicate payment made for the same booking
  • Technical error resulting in incorrect charge

⚠️ Partial Refund (50%)

  • Booking cancelled less than 24 hours but more than 2 hours before the service
  • Service partially completed due to unforeseen circumstances
  • Quality of service was below the agreed standard (verified by our team)

❌ No Refund

  • Service successfully completed and accepted by the customer
  • Cancellation made less than 2 hours before service time
  • Customer was unavailable at the time of service without prior notice
  • Refund requested more than 7 days after the service date

Refund Timeline

  • Initiation: Within 24–48 hours of approval
  • UPI / Wallets: 1–3 business days
  • Credit / Debit Cards: 5–7 business days
  • Net Banking: 5–7 business days

Refunds are processed to the original payment method only.

How to Request a Refund

  1. Log in to your HamaraService account
  2. Go to My Bookings and select the relevant booking
  3. Tap Request Refund and describe your issue
  4. Our team will review and respond within 24 business hours

Alternatively, email support@hamaraservice.com with your Booking ID and a brief description.

🔒 Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal data

Effective: June 2025

Who We Are

HamaraService is a home services platform operated from Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, India. Our website is hamaraservice.com. We connect customers with verified home service professionals.

For privacy-related questions, contact: privacy@hamaraservice.com

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account details: Name, email address, mobile number
  • Booking details: Service type, address, preferred date and time
  • Payment details: Processed securely via Razorpay — we do not store card numbers
  • Communications: Messages sent through our support system

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device type, browser, operating system
  • Approximate location (for matching nearby providers)
  • Usage patterns and pages visited on our platform

How We Use Your Information

  • To process bookings and match you with service providers
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders and updates via SMS/email
  • To process payments and issue refunds
  • To improve our platform, services and customer experience
  • To comply with legal obligations under Indian law
  • To send promotional offers — only with your consent, unsubscribe anytime

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Service Providers: Your name, phone and address are shared with the assigned provider to complete your booking
  • Payment Processor: Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant) for secure payment processing
  • Firebase (Google): Our database infrastructure for secure data storage
  • Government Authorities: When required by law

Data Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS
  • Firebase Realtime Database with role-based access rules
  • Passwords are never stored — we use Firebase Authentication
  • Payments processed by Razorpay (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified)

Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update incorrect information via your profile
  • Deletion: Request account deletion — email privacy@hamaraservice.com
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing at any time

Cookies

We use minimal cookies for session management and analytics (Google Analytics). No third-party advertising cookies are used. You can clear cookies via your browser settings at any time.
