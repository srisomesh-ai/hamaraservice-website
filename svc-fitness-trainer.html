Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need gym equipment at home?›
No — trainers bring resistance bands and design effective bodyweight workouts. For strength training, basic dumbbells or resistance bands at home help, but are not mandatory.
Is the trainer certified?›
Yes — all trainers are certified (CPT, ACE, ISSA, or equivalent Indian certifications). You can request certification details at booking.
How much space is needed for a home workout?›
A 6x6 feet clear space is enough for most workouts. The trainer will adapt the session to the space available in your home.
Can I train if I have a health condition?›
Yes — mention your condition at booking. The trainer will design a safe, appropriate workout. For serious conditions, medical clearance from your doctor is recommended before starting.
Can a family member join my session?›
Yes — for Zumba and group sessions, you can include family members. For personalised training (weight loss, muscle, rehab), individual sessions give better results.
How soon will I see results?›
With consistent training (3–5 sessions/week), most people see visible improvement in 4–6 weeks. Weight loss, strength, and flexibility vary based on diet and consistency.
Can I get the same trainer every session?›
Yes — for regular bookings and monthly packages, the same trainer is assigned for continuity and better progress tracking.
What if I am not happy with the trainer?›
Contact us within 24 hours and we will arrange a replacement trainer at no extra charge.
