Frequently Asked Questions
Should I provide cleaning supplies?›
Yes. The maid uses your broom, mop, bucket, and cleaning liquid. Please keep them ready before she arrives.
Can I book a regular daily maid?›
Yes. After your first booking you can set a recurring schedule — daily, alternate days, or weekly at a discounted rate.
Are maids background verified?›
Yes. Every maid on our platform is identity-verified and has been personally reviewed before being onboarded.
What if I'm not satisfied?›
Raise a concern within 24 hours and we'll arrange a free re-visit or process a refund — no questions asked.
How many rooms does one visit cover?›
A 2–4 hour slot covers up to a 3 BHK comfortably. For larger homes, book an extended 4–6 hour slot.
