Frequently Asked Questions
Does the mechanic bring spare parts?›
Mechanics carry common parts like spark plugs, fuses, and bulbs. For engine oil, filters, batteries, and tyres — cost is confirmed before purchasing. You can also provide your own parts.
Can the mechanic do an oil change at home?›
Yes — select "Engine Oil & Filter Change". The mechanic brings the drain equipment. Engine oil and filter are purchased separately — cost confirmed before work begins.
My bike won't start — what should I book?›
Switch to Bike and select "Battery / Starter Issue" or "General Service / Checkup". The mechanic will diagnose whether it's the battery, starter motor, or fuel system.
Can I book for both car and bike in one visit?›
Car and bike mechanics are different specialists. Book them as separate services — each vehicle gets the right certified mechanic.
Which car and bike brands do you service?›
Cars: Maruti, Hyundai, Tata, Mahindra, Honda, Toyota, Kia, MG, and all others. Bikes: Royal Enfield, Honda, TVS, Bajaj, Yamaha, Hero, KTM, and all others.
Is there a diagnosis charge?›
The visit and diagnosis charge is included in the service fee. Parts are priced separately and only after your approval.
Is there a warranty on the work done?›
Yes — 7-day workmanship guarantee. If the same issue reoccurs within 7 days due to the mechanic's work, the revisit is free.
Can the mechanic tow my vehicle if it can't be repaired on the spot?›
Towing is not included in this service. If the vehicle needs workshop-level repair, the mechanic will advise and you can arrange towing separately.
