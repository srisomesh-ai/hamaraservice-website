Frequently Asked Questions
How long does deep cleaning take?›
1 BHK: 4–6 hours · 2 BHK: 6–8 hours · 3 BHK: 8–10 hours. Larger homes are quoted separately.
Do I need to provide cleaning materials?›
No. The professional team brings all tools, chemicals, machines, and equipment.
Is it safe for children and pets?›
Yes. Safe and approved cleaning products are used. Keep children and pets away during the process and for 30 minutes after.
How often should deep cleaning be done?›
Recommended every 3–6 months for a healthy, hygienic home.
Will stains be removed completely?›
Most stains can be significantly reduced or removed. Permanent damage stains may not fully come out.
Do I need to vacate the house?›
Not always. Some areas may be temporarily inaccessible during cleaning.
Can I customize the package?›
Yes. Use the calculator above to select specific rooms and add-ons like mattress shampooing, carpet cleaning, or balcony cleaning.
