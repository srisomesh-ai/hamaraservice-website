Frequently Asked Questions
Are the guards police verified?›
Yes — all guards are background-checked and police-verified before deployment. You can request verification documents at booking.
Can I book a guard for a single day?›
Yes — single day or single shift bookings are available. You can also book for multiple consecutive days at a discounted rate.
What does the guard bring?›
Guards arrive in uniform and carry a mobile phone for communication. Walkie-talkies, torches, and batons can be added as optional equipment at booking.
Can I book multiple guards for an event?›
Yes — set the number of guards needed when booking. For large events with 10+ guards, contact us directly for a custom quote.
Is armed security available?›
Armed and specialised security is a separate service requiring additional licensing. Contact us directly to arrange armed security for high-risk requirements.
Can the guard stay overnight?›
Yes — select the Night Guard (8-hour shift) for overnight coverage from 10 PM to 6 AM. For full 24-hour coverage, book both a day shift and a night shift guard.
What happens if the guard doesn't show up?›
We have a replacement guarantee — if a guard cannot report, a replacement is arranged immediately. Your security is never left unattended.
Is food and accommodation provided for the guard?›
For short deployments (8–12 hours), basic meals are the customer's courtesy. For multi-day deployments, accommodation and meal arrangements should be discussed at booking.
What is the difference between a security guard and a bouncer?›
Security guards focus on monitoring, patrol, and gate management. Bouncers are physically trained for crowd control, entry management, handling disruptive guests, and close protection at events and venues.
Can I book bouncers for a private party or wedding?›
Yes — select "Event Bouncer" for weddings, private parties, and functions. For VIP protection or high-profile events, select "VIP / Personal Bouncer" instead.
